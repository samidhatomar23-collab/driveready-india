Privacy Policy

DriveReady India · Last updated: 24 November 2026

DriveReady India ("the app", "we") is a free, ad-free practice app that helps people prepare for the Indian learner's licence test. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app has no sign-up or login. We do not ask for your name, phone number, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal data.

2. Information stored on your device

To work offline and remember your progress, the app saves some information only on your own device: your chosen language and settings, lesson and test progress, scores, XP, streaks and test history. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else. If you clear the app's data or uninstall the app, it is deleted.

3. Advertising and tracking

The app contains no advertising. We do not use analytics, tracking tools or third-party advertising services, and we do not sell or share your data.

4. Permissions

The app does not request any special device permissions. It needs internet access only to load or update itself; after that it can work offline.

5. Children

The app is intended for people preparing for a driving licence (18 years and above) and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone.

6. Contacting us

If you email us, we will see your email address and your message, and we will use them only to reply to you. We do not add them to any mailing list.

7. Disclaimer

DriveReady India is an independent practice app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any government body, RTO or transport department. Rules and test questions can differ by state; please check your state transport department for the latest official information.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
