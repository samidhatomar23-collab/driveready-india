HOW TO PUT THIS UPDATE ON YOUR APP (about 5 minutes)

1. Unzip this file on your PC. You get a folder called driveready-india-multilang.
2. Open your GitHub repository page (driveready-india) in the browser.
3. Click "Add file" > "Upload files".
4. Open the unzipped folder, select EVERYTHING inside it (all files and folders), and drag them into the GitHub page.
   (Do not upload a folder named node_modules - it is not in the zip.)
5. Wait for the upload to finish, write "Add new topics and questions" in the message box, click "Commit changes".
6. Vercel updates your site by itself in 1-2 minutes. Open https://driveready-india.vercel.app, close the app completely and open it again once (so the new version loads).
7. In the app: Profile > Language, or the small language button on every question, to switch between English, Hindi, Marathi, Telugu and Tamil.

NOTE: Upload every file and folder from the zip, including public/signs (new sign pictures) and src/data (questions).
Do NOT upload your signing key or password files to GitHub.
