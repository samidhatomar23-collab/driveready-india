# Content update (Nov 2026)

* The app now has 400 questions in 10 topics. All topics are open (Road signs, Right of way, Signals & markings, Speed & overtaking, Parking, Documents, Fines, Safe driving, First aid & accidents, Vehicle basics).
* New questions are written in English and Hindi. Marathi, Telugu and Tamil show the English text for the new questions (the older questions and all screens stay in all five languages). Translating the new questions into those three languages is the next step.
* Sources: Motor Vehicles Act 1988 as amended (India Code text) for fines and duties; Motor Vehicles (Driving) Regulations 2017 for road rules; the official Parivahan question bank (STALL_QB_ENGLISH_NEW.pdf) for questions, sign pictures and markings. Each question names its source. Fine amounts are the central-Act amounts; states can change them.
* Pictures from the Parivahan PDF: each picture question was checked against its picture. PDF items whose answer key did not match the picture were left out (Q8, Q20, Q38, Q48, Q90, Q146-158, Q243, Q246, Q258, Q426, Q429, Q431, Q434, Q435).
* Please confirm you may reuse the Parivahan pictures and text before publishing on Play Store.
* All Hindi, Marathi, Telugu and Tamil text was written by Claude and should be checked by a native speaker (see TRANSLATION_NOTES.md).
* Fixed: Mock Test now submits automatically when time runs out on the last question, and the phone Back button asks before leaving a Mock Test.
